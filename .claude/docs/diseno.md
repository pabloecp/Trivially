# Design rules

The look comes from the "Trivially rediseño" canvas (1 · Inicio, 2 · ¿Qué quieren jugar?, 4 · Ajustes de Trivia and "Piezas y reglas"). It lives in `frontend/src/styles/look.css`, loaded last (`main.jsx`): that file is how things look (tokens, buttons, cards, panels, type), the older files keep how they're laid out. Reuse its tokens and each game's (`--tv-*-edge`, `tv-c-*`) instead of loose colours.

## The layout
- **Arriba la info, al centro el juego, abajo tu grupo y la acción.** Inicio, the group's room and the search are one frame (`HomeFrame.jsx`): the top bar, the game's stage in the middle, the group's bar at the bottom of the window (`.tv-lobby`, `.tv-gbar`). On phones they stack: stage, the boxes, then the bar's zones one under the other.
- **Phones: your sala up top, the action at the foot** (≤ 760 px, `GroupBar`'s `SalaMenu` and `ShareLink`, `look.css`). TU GRUPO leaves the bottom bar: it's a short grey chip in the top bar, beside "Volver" (`.tv-chip--sala`: your sala's faces, yours first with the yellow ring, and how many are in it; without a group your Blob and "Unirse"), and a tap opens the whole zone under it like the volume's panel (`.tv-sala-panel`: code and "Copiar", "Invitar", "Salir del grupo"; or "¿Te invitaron?" and the code's box). The bottom bar sticks to the foot of the window while the page scrolls: "Vas a jugar" (saying "· Pública" / "· Privada", changed in "¿Qué quieren jugar?"; no "Pública / Privada" switch there) and, in one row, "Enlace" (grey, copies the invite link `/grupo/CODE`) and the yellow button. The stage's "Código de la sala" box is hidden there (it would repeat it).
- **Inicio fills the window** (computers): the stage runs from under the top bar to the group's bar, 14 px apart, no gaps above or below; the right column is 340 px; the card has the game's `description` under its name.
- **The top bar has no logo nor address**: on the left only the way back ("Volver", or the match's "X"; with a sala of only you on Inicio, "Volver" leaves it); on the right, in this order, the owners' panel (its gear, owners only), the volume, "Amigos" with how many are online in yellow (accounts) and your Blob with your name. No elo (it's in the "Tu elo" box) and no sala code (it's in the address, `/grupo/CODE`, and in the group's bar).
- **The Blob's colour** (`lib/blobColors.js`, checked again by the server in `db/store.js`): white for everyone, Celeste, Rosa and Menta with an account, Dorado only for owners. Picked only on the profile ("Color de tu Blob": every colour like an inventory, the one you wear in the site's blue, the ones you can't use dimmed with a lock, and a tap on one says how to get it). No Google photo; any other saved avatar shows white.
- **The group's bar** (`GroupBar`) has three zones, each with a small grey uppercase label (`.tv-gbar-cap`) over a flat `--tv-surface-2` box (`.tv-gbar-box`, 18 px corners): TU GRUPO (faces, who decides, the code and its copy button, "Invitar", "Salir del grupo"; without a group "Juegas solo", "¿Te invitaron?" and the code's box), PARTIDA ("Pública" / "Privada", the chosen one in the site's blue with its edge, `.tv-gbar-seg`, and "Vas a jugar") and the yellow button. Until a game is chosen JUGAR is grey with a lock.
- **One yellow button per screen** (JUGAR, EMPEZAR, Listo, "Volver a jugar", "Volver al grupo"…): `.tv-btn--primary`. Everything else is grey (`.tv-btn--neutral`, `.tv-iconbtn`), except the white "Unirse" beside the code's box (`.tv-btn--white`) and what leaves or takes something away, red (`.tv-btn--bad`: "Salir del grupo" and the confirm of its dialog).
- **Settings hide behind a summary**: in the stage's right column, a private match's summary (up to three lines) with the "Ajustes" button under it, which opens the panel (`SettingsPanel`, its `card`). A public match has no settings to change: its column says how many are playing and its fixed rules in a line, no "Ajustes". On Inicio a private one's "Ajustes" makes the match first and opens its settings there; a group's player who isn't its leader sees "Los elige …" instead of the button.

## A match (every game)
- **The same frame for the eight games** (`components/home/MatchFrame.jsx`, styles at the end of `look.css`, the "Partidas" boards): the page fills the window like Inicio. The **top bar** has "Salir" (asks first), the game's chip in its colour with how this round is played ("Trivia · Cerradas", `matchMode`), then "RONDA 4 DE 10", the sala's code and one segment per round (`RoundSegments`: done blue, now white), and on the right Amigos, the elo, your Blob and the volume.
- **The round's card** (`MatchCard`, `.tv-qc`): the game's colour, its icon big and faint in the corner; first row the mode or topic on a light pill (plus darker pills: difficulty, points, Farsante's three steps) and the time left on the right ("12 s", "Empieza en 3", "Respuesta"; red when little is left); under it what to do in one or two lines (`dir`); the question at the bottom (`MatchQuestion`, smaller when long; "Encuentra Perú" big) and the clock's white bar. Three fixed heights, the same every round: s 180 px (Letra, Encuentra el país · Nombre, Campo de minas, Lluvia), m 220 px (the rest), l 260 px. The verdict of a round goes on its first row ("¡Correcto! +840", "Incorrecto", "Se acabó el tiempo").
- **Under the card**, what the game answers with: Trivia's and the song's four options (grey, the chosen one blue with "Tu respuesta", at the reveal the right one green and a wrong pick red), a panel (`.tv-mpanel`: "TU RESPUESTA", the box to write in with its blue ring and the yellow "Enviar", "Saltar" as a quiet link), Rango's bar, the map with "Confirmar pin" yellow on it, the board, the rain, the king and the questions to pick, the answers to vote.
- **The scoreboard** (`PartyPanel scores`; the same look during a game's roulette and tutorial, `.tv-room.is-intro`, so it doesn't change when the rounds start): "MARCADOR" and a pill with how the round goes ("Respondieron 3/4", "Confirmaron", "Fijaron", "Siguen", "Votaron", "En juego"), a flat box per player (position, Blob with a green dot once they answered, your name alone (never a "tú"), a quiet line: "Respondió", "Confirmó", "Pensando…", "Buscando…", "6 aciertos", "Ya votó", "Rey" in yellow, "Retador"; points with a dot for thousands), you with a yellow ring. No code, chat nor crown in it.
- **The bottom bar** (`MatchBar`): everyone's Blob (a green dot once they answered), who the round still waits for, you by your name too ("Faltan Pablo y Lynz por confirmar", "Faltan 3 por responder"; Campo de minas who stepped on a mine, Lluvia who took the last drop, Rey "Nadie le ha quitado la corona a Lynz todavía"), then "Chat" (a panel over the page), "Reportar" and "Pausar" (the host), grey. No "Terminar" there: the host's "Salir" already ends the match for everyone (it stays on a phone beside a central device).
- **Each game, as its board:** the card's "what to do" is the board's own line while the round is open (the game's hint before and after). Encuentra el país: "+" and "−" on the map top left (no strip of regions), the sea and land in the game's teal, "Confirmar pin" always so named; Sombra and Bandera with the silhouette or the flag beside "Encuentra este país". Rango: "TU RESPUESTA" and the number big on its right, the bar (filled in orange, a white square knob) between "−" and "+", "Fijar respuesta". Campo de minas: no counters over the board. Lluvia: the drops in the game's blue with "5 · difícil", the box to write with "Enviar" on the sky's foot. Rey: the king big on the left ("El rey · eligió esta") and "RETADORES" on the right. Farsante: "Escribir · Votar · Revelar" on the card, your answer with a pencil and "La escribiste tú". Letra: the gap a pink dashed box with "?".
- **Phones:** the top bar keeps "Salir", "TRIVIA · RONDA 4 DE 10" with its segments, and your Blob; the card (170/200/230 px), the answers in one column, the scoreboard as one strip of faces and points, and the bar's buttons side by side.

## Colours and surfaces
- Page `--tv-bg` #0a0b0c with its faint pattern of triangles, circles, crosses and waves (`--tv-page-bg`).
- Big panels (a panel over the page, the group's bar) `--tv-surface` #141517, 20–24 px corners, no coloured border, no edge; over the page only a soft drop shadow, and the page behind is darkened (60 % black).
- Boxes inside them (option rows, the code's box) `--tv-surface-2` #1c1d20, flat, 12–14 px corners.
- Games: Trivia `--tv-qm`, Adivina la canción `--p-music-*`, Encuentra el país `--p-world-*`, Rango `--tv-hm`, Campo de minas `--tv-mn`, Lluvia `--tv-rn`, Rey `--tv-ry`, Farsante `--tv-fs`; each with its darker `--edge`, never black.
- Chosen (an option of a row, a category): the site's blue `--tv-pick` (#2f6bff) with `0 3px 0 --tv-pick-edge` (#1a3c99), white text; the others have no fill. Text links `--tv-link` (#5b8cff), bold, never underlined.

## Type
- Titles (a game's name, a panel's title): Outfit 800, tighter (`--tv-title`, `-0.03em`).
- Buttons, game names and labels: Baloo 2, 700–800 (`--tv-display`).
- Quiet text (subtitles, notes): Plus Jakarta Sans (`--tv-small`), grey `--tv-text-3`.

## Pieces
- **The game's stage** (`ModeStage`, `.tv-stage`) is the middle of Inicio, of the group's room and of the search: the game's **card** in its colour (`.tv-stage-card`, flat: only its "Ajustes" is tapped; 24 px corners) and, beside it, two dark boxes (`.tv-stage-aside`), the whole as wide as the group's bar and coming in as one panel. **The card:** the game's icon on a white square and, in one row beside it, who the match is for in small (`.tv-stage-aud`: globe "Partida pública" or lock "Partida privada") over the game's name, always on one line ("…" if even the smallest size doesn't fit). At the bottom of the card (`.tv-stage-bottom`) three short flat lines (`.tv-stage-lines`, an icon on a lighter square and a few words): how it's played (the game's `tagline`), the settings (`settings.summary`; on Inicio a private match has none, never "Ajustes por defecto"), and how many play it now (a white dot sending out a ring). In a private match that last line is the glass "Ajustes" button (or who chooses them, with a lock). A public match says nothing about its rules. The search shows its rows there instead (time, queue, players found). The game's icon, big and faint, is cut by the bottom right corner. **Beside it:** "Tu elo" (the number with a yellow star and "+38 esta semana", green, red when it went down) with **your character** in its empty right side: "Blob" (`components/home/Blob.jsx`, an SVG: a round white body a little wider at the top, a thick black line and two black oval eyes, nothing else), floating a little; under it "Reto del día" (a yellow square with a flame, the goal and how, "4/10" and a yellow bar), and "Amigos en línea" (up to three online friends with "Unirme" to an open group or "Invitar" to yours; without an account, a line saying what it's for). With a sala, "Código de la sala" (big, "Copiar") takes the challenge's place and "En tu sala · N" the friends': one flat box per member like the scoreboard's, yours with the yellow ring. The elo, the players and the challenge are example numbers until those systems exist (`lib/gameStats.js`). Narrower than 860 px the card goes across the top and the boxes under it (one under the other below 560 px). A Clásico's card is in the logo's look (`.is-classic`: dark in the logo's coloured frame, the logo in place of the icon), with no boxes beside it. **No game chosen yet** (Inicio on a new device, `plan.game` null): the grey card with the logo, "Aún no has elegido / Elige un juego", a line of what there is, three lines and the screen's yellow button "Elegir juego" (opens "¿Qué quieren jugar?"), with only "Amigos en línea" beside it. A private match before its host picks: "Elige un juego" alone in `--tv-surface`.
- **The games' cards** (`GameCard`, `.tv-gcard`): tapped, so with their edge (`0 4px 0 var(--edge)`); icon on a white square top left, name and tagline, the faint icon cut by the corner; the chosen one with a white ring. 4 columns on a computer, 2 on phones.
- **"Clásico"** (`.tv-ccard`, first in "¿Qué quieren jugar?", above "Juego libre"): one tile half the panel wide (two of the games’ columns; the whole width on phones), like the one it had on the old room screen: black, tapped (edge `--tv-logo-shadow`), in a 5 px frame of the logo’s five coloured slices (`--logo-1…5`). "Clásico" and "Primero en ganar dos juegos" at the top left (`.tv-ccard-name`, `-sub`, which stop short of the logo) and the logo (`AppIcon`) big on the right, tilted and cut by the bottom right corner (`.tv-ccard-logo`), wiggling on hover. Chosen, the games' white ring (`.is-selected`). Its big card (Inicio, its lobby, its search) is the same stage in the logo's look (`.tv-stage.is-classic`: dark in the logo's coloured frame, the logo where a game's badge goes and "Primero en ganar dos juegos" under its name; its tutorial says how it goes).
- **"Vas a jugar"** (the group's bar, `.tv-gbar-plan`): the same card (`.tv-gcard` and its badge, name and faint icon), in the game's colour and with its icon, smaller and always 250 px wide on a computer whatever the game's name (long ones cut with "…"), "Vas a jugar" in small over the name, a chevron on the right; grey "Elige un juego" with no game. The Clásico's is in the logo's look (`.is-classic`: black in the logo's coloured frame, the logo where a game's badge goes). It's where the game is changed (no strip or list of games on the screen). Beside it, in the same PARTIDA box (`.tv-gbar-plan-row`), "Pública" / "Privada".
- **Panels over the page** (`Modal.jsx`, `.tv-modal`): title and a grey X; a body that scrolls and an optional foot (Restablecer left, Listo right). On phones a sheet from the bottom. Inside `.tv-app` (its styles reach them).
- **Pills** (`.tv-pill`, "Partida pública / Partida privada") and **tabs** (`SettingsTabs`: icon and title, a grey summary under, the open one with a 2 px blue line): flat.
- **No profile photos: every player's face is their Blob** (`Avatar.jsx` draws `Blob.jsx` in the colour of their `avatar`, with a black line), at any size; yours in the group's bar has a yellow ring.
- **The profile** keeps only what matters: your Blob, name, @username and two tags at most ("Owner", "Registrado"); "Estadísticas" with Victorias and Aciertos and "Color de tu Blob" under them; "Tu cuenta" (name, @username, Google to link, Spotify for owners, sign out). No match history.
- **Results** fit the panel with no scrolling: side by side the cards give up height when it's short, and below 860 px of height the head, podium, rows and buttons are a little smaller (`home.css`).

## How big panels move
- Every big panel (the game's stage, the group's bar, a panel over the page, the room's column, a public match's card, the central lobby, profile cards) comes in all together, nothing inside it animating apart, up from below: `--tv-box-in` (`tvBoxIn`, in `home.css`'s `:root`). Going out is the reverse, all together: `--tv-box-out` (`tvBoxOut`, 0.3 s). Reduced motion: none.

## Shadows
- **With a shadow** (a solid edge in the piece's own darker colour, `box-shadow: 0 3px 0 var(--edge)`): only what is tapped and does something. Buttons (`.tv-btn`, `.tv-iconbtn`, the top bar's `.tv-chip`), answers (`.tv-option`, `.tv-quiz-opt`), cells (`.tv-mines-cell`), the games' cards (`.tv-gcard`), chosen options (`.tv-optrow-btn` pressed), coloured icons. On hover it rises (1–2 px) and the edge grows; pressed, it goes down and the edge disappears.
- The edge is always the piece's colour, darker: `--edge` of its `tv-c-*`, `--p-accent-edge` (#b39200) for the yellow, `--tv-neutral-edge` (#191919) for grey, `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.22)` for a white button on a coloured card (`0.18` for the game card's glass "Ajustes"), #b5b8c0 for the white `.tv-btn--white` on the dark, `--p-bad-edge` for the red `.tv-btn--bad`. Never a plain black edge under a coloured or white button.
- Quiet icon buttons with no background (`.tv-iconbtn--quiet`, a row's X, a playlist row's tools) stay flat.
- Buttons are rounded rectangles: square icon ones 40 px with 12 px corners (36 px / 10 px small), text ones 12 px corners (10 px small, 14 px big). Only avatars, dots, switches and labels are round.
- **Flat:** boxes to write in (a coloured ring on focus), pills and tabs (the chosen one a lighter fill, `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`, or a line), cards and rows, labels, avatars, covers.
- **Never** an inset shadow at the top (`inset 0 3px 0 …`).
- Text buttons and profile links are never underlined: they brighten on hover.
