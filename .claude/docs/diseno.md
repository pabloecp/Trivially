# Design rules

The look comes from the "Trivially rediseño" canvas (1 · Inicio, 2 · ¿Qué quieren jugar?, 4 · Ajustes de Trivia and "Piezas y reglas"). It lives in `frontend/src/styles/look.css`, loaded last (`main.jsx`): that file is how things look (tokens, buttons, cards, panels, type), the older files keep how they're laid out. Reuse its tokens and each game's (`--tv-*-edge`, `tv-c-*`) instead of loose colours.

## The layout
- **Arriba la info, al centro el juego (con tu grupo al lado), abajo la acción.** Inicio, the group's room and the search are one frame (`HomeFrame.jsx`): the top bar, the game's stage in the middle (your group in the boxes beside the card), the bar at the bottom of the window (`.tv-lobby`, `.tv-gbar`: the day's challenge, "Vas a jugar", JUGAR). On phones they stack: stage, the boxes, then the bar's zones one under the other.
- **Phones: your group up top, the action at the foot** (≤ 760 px, `GroupBar`'s `GroupMenu`, `look.css`). TU GRUPO leaves the bottom bar for the top bar, beside "Volver", as one grey chip: your group's faces (`.tv-chip--sala`: up to three, yours first and on top with the yellow ring, then how many are in it; yours alone without a number), whose tap opens "Tu grupo" (`GroupSheet`, the sheet from the bottom: the code, who is in it, friends to invite, and on phones the red "Salir del grupo" at its foot, which closes it and asks first). The code is never in the top bar. Without a group there is no chip ("Invitar" beside the card makes the group). The top bar keeps every button in that row (Volver, the group, the volume, Amigos, your Blob), never squeezed: 36 px buttons on phones, Amigos only its icon (no count; its requests keep their red badge) and your Blob without your name; owners have no volume button there, it's the first block ("Volumen") of their panel (the gear), so every other player sees the volume button and owners see the gear. Narrower than 380 px tighter gaps; at 320 px 34 px buttons. The bottom bar sticks to the foot of the window while the page scrolls, its panel running on down to the window's foot (nothing shows under it); while it only waits a moment (switching "Pública" / "Privada") the yellow button can't be pressed but doesn't dim: "Vas a jugar" the whole width, and the yellow button under it, the whole width too.
- **Inicio fills the window** (computers): the stage runs from under the top bar to the group's bar, 14 px apart, no gaps above or below; the right column is 340 px; the card has the game's `description` under its name.
- **The top bar has no logo nor address**: on the left only the way back ("Volver", or the match's "X"; none on Inicio, also while a room waits there); on the right, in this order, the owners' panel (its gear, owners only), the volume, "Amigos" with how many are online in yellow (accounts) and your Blob with your name. No elo (it's in the "Tu elo" box) and no group code (it's only in "Tu grupo").
- **The Blob's colour** (`lib/blobColors.js`, checked again by the server in `db/store.js`): white for everyone; with an account the colours of the games (Trivia, Canción, Mundo, Lluvia, Rango, Farsante, Minas). No owners' colour (their old gold shows white; the old account colours show as their new ones). Picked only on the profile ("Color de tu Blob": the eight in two full rows of four, like an inventory, the one you wear in the site's blue, the ones you can't use dimmed with a lock, and a tap on one says how to get it). No Google photo; any other saved avatar shows white.
- **The Blob's skin** (`Blob.jsx`): drawn in layers, all inside its black line: the colour, a shadow inside (a crescent low on the right in the colour's darker tone, `shade` in `blobColors.js`), a light high on the left, and an empty layer for future shines or reflections. Everyone sees "Sombra interior"; an owner in debug view can try "Plano" and "Luz y sombra" ("Estilo de los Blobs" in the owners' panel, `useBlobStyle`). No skin has an animation.
- **The Blob's moods** (`mood` on `Blob`/`Avatar`, `look.css`): "victoria", a jump straight up every 2.8 s looking up (first place on the podium; tied, all of them); "derrota", it sinks a little while its eyes drop, look down to each side and half close (second and third on the podium); "neutral", only the eyes look left and right now and then, each Blob at its own moment (the scoreboard of a match and of the results, a group's list in "Tu grupo", the big one beside your elo). Everywhere else (buttons, stacks of faces, the profile) it's still. Only an owner can keep them all still, on their device ("Reducir movimiento" in the owners' panel, `useBlobsStill`); the device's own reduced-motion setting doesn't stop them.
- **The group's bar** (`GroupBar`) has three zones, each with a small grey uppercase label (`.tv-gbar-cap`) over a flat `--tv-surface-2` box (`.tv-gbar-box`, 18 px corners): RETO DEL DÍA (`DailyZone`: the yellow square with the flame, the goal over its yellow bar, "1/3"; without an account one line saying what it gives; the group is beside the card, `grupos.md`), PARTIDA ("Vas a jugar" alone: there's no "Pública" / "Privada" switch on the bar, that's chosen in "¿Qué quieren jugar?" with the game) and the yellow button. Until a game is chosen JUGAR is grey with a lock.
- **One yellow button per screen** (JUGAR, the same for a public or a private match, Listo, "Volver a jugar", "Volver al grupo"…): `.tv-btn--primary`. Everything else is grey (`.tv-btn--neutral`, `.tv-iconbtn`), except what leaves or takes something away, red (`.tv-btn--bad`: "Salir del grupo" and the confirm of its dialog).
- **Settings hide behind a summary**: in the stage's right column, a private match's summary (up to three lines) with the "Ajustes" button under it, which opens the panel (`SettingsPanel`, its `card`). A public match has no settings to change: its column says how many are playing and its fixed rules in a line, no "Ajustes". On Inicio a private one's "Ajustes" makes the match first and opens its settings there; a group's player who isn't its leader sees "Los elige …" instead of the button.

## A match
- **The same frame for the eight games** (`components/home/MatchFrame.jsx`, styles in `look.css`, the "Partidas" boards). The page fills the window like Inicio. The **top bar** has "Salir" (asks first), then "RONDA 4 DE 10" (the game and how the round is played are on the card, not here) and one segment per round (`RoundSegments`: done blue, now white), and on the right Amigos, the elo, your Blob and the volume.
- **The round's card** (`MatchCard`, `.tv-qc`): the game's colour, its icon big and faint in the corner; first row the game and how this round is played on a light pill with the game's icon ("Trivia · Cerradas", "Adivina la canción · Letra", "Campo de minas · Carrera"; the name alone for a game with one way, `matchMode`), "Desempate" on a darker pill in a tiebreak (`tag`) (plus darker pills: difficulty, points, Farsante's three steps) and the time left on the right ("12 s", "Empieza en 3", "Respuesta"; red when little is left); no line of what to do under it (the question and the answers say it); the question at the bottom (`MatchQuestion`), with an image on its left when there is one (`media`: the flag, the silhouette; it takes the height left between the first row and the clock, 84 px at most, so it never reaches the first row), and the clock's white bar. **Two sizes**, each never changing within a game: the normal one, 240 px (Trivia, Adivina la canción, Rango, Rey de la colina, Farsante), and the small one (`small`), 150 px, for the games played on a map or a board, which need the room (Encuentra el país, Campo de minas, Lluvia). On a phone see "Phones". The question is 28 px (24 px in the small one) and never cut: one that doesn't fit gets as much smaller as it takes (`MatchQuestion` measures it, 14 px at the least, again if the card changes size). **Its text never changes at the reveal** ("¿Qué palabra sigue?" stays, it doesn't become "Así seguía"; "¿Dónde está Perú?" doesn't become "Perú"). Adivina la canción asks "¿Qué canción suena?" (with Abiertas "¿Qué canción suena? Escribe el título").
- **Between rounds nothing flies in again:** the card and the panels under it come in with `--tv-box-in` only in the first round of a match; from the second on they stay in their place and only fade in (0.25 s) with the new round (`html.tv-round-next`, set by `MatchCard`).
- **Confetti** only where it means something: a streak of 3 or more right answers (`RoundVerdict`), a cleared board or a won tiebreak in Campo de minas, and the results screens. Not on every right answer, nor on every round won in Lluvia or Rey de la colina.
- **The round's result**, the same in every game (`RoundVerdict`): on the card's first row, what happened in white with green letters ("¡Correcto!", "¡Dentro!"), red when wrong ("Incorrecto"), dark when time ran out, white with the game's colour when close ("A 320 km", "Te faltaron 349"); the points apart in a dark label ("+900", no "pts"), and the streak ("Racha de 3"). Rectangles of 30 px (26 px on a phone), never pills. On a phone the topic and the streak step aside at the reveal, so it never gets cut.
- **Under the card**, what the game answers with, and **how you answer decides how the answer shows**:
  - **Options** (Trivia and the song, Cerradas; Farsante's vote): grey, the chosen one blue with "Tu respuesta"; at the reveal they stay where they were, the right one green and a wrong pick red. Cerradas' four options sit on a dark grey panel (`.tv-opt-panel`, 18 × 20 px around them; 12 px on a phone), so they read as one block under the card (not on a central device nor on a phone beside one).
  - **Writing** (Trivia and the song, Abiertas; Rey de la colina): the writing bar, the same in every game: the box with its blue ring, the yellow "Enviar" and, at the right end, "Saltar" grey and as wide (150 px each; `AnswerSkip`, only where you may skip: the first tap turns it red with "Sin puntos", the second skips, always the same size), 64 px on one row, in the middle of its panel. Adivina la canción's suggestions open under the box, exactly as wide, over the panel and never past its bottom (they scroll inside). On a phone the box has its own row (52 px) and "Enviar" and "Saltar" share the one under it, with their words; without "Saltar", "Enviar" is only its icon beside the box. Lluvia uses the same bar, without "Saltar". At the reveal the bar turns into **the answer's card** (`AnswerCard`) in its place, in the middle of the panel and with no box of its own (only over a map or a board does it have one): what it is ("La respuesta", "La canción", "La palabra"), the answer big, a line under it (the song's artists and year) and how it went for you, green or red ("Escribiste “Peru”"); a song's cover on its left.
  - **Rango's bar**: the number big over the bar; at the reveal the number turns into the same answer's card, the bar keeps everyone's marks.
  - **A map or a board** (Encuentra el país, Campo de minas, Lluvia): at the reveal the same answer's card floats in its middle (`floating`), as a panel over the page (no line, a soft drop shadow), the board in sight around it, darkened.
  - Adivina la canción · Letra: the card is the small one (like the games on a board), saying "¿Qué palabra sigue?", and a notch on its time bar marks where the song is cut, with nothing written: 7 px wide (5 px on a phone), in the game's darker colour, no outline. The lyrics go under it in the answers' panel, as big as the card's "¿Qué palabra sigue?" (24 px; 21 px on a phone), every line the same size (the lines centred, and when they don't fit, at the bottom with those above fading out): the one being sung white, the others grey, the gap an empty pink slot at the end of the last one (a light pink fill with a pink line under it, breathing softly while its line is sung, never a "?"), filled pink with the word the moment the round ends. With Cerradas the lyrics (three lines tall) and the four options share that one dark grey panel, the options laid out as in Trivia in all the room left under the lyrics; with Abiertas the writing bar goes in the same panel, under the lyrics; at the reveal the answer's card ("La palabra") goes on top of the lyrics, the two together in the middle of the panel. (The lyrics leave the card here on purpose: in Letra they are the whole game.)
  - Trivia with Abiertas (not a flag question): over the writing bar, five still question marks in Trivia's blue, as big as the song's bars (240 × 110; 150 × 80 on a phone), and "<topic> · escribe la respuesta" under them, so the panel isn't empty; they go with the keyboard up. A flag question has its flag there instead.
  - Adivina la canción · Título: the card never has the song's bars. With Abiertas they go big in the panel, in pink, and the writing bar under them. The suggestions open upwards, over the bars. With the keyboard up the bars give way.
- **Also under the card**: the map with "Confirmar pin" yellow on it, the board (Campo de minas: its cells inside the dark grey panel with the same room around them as Cerradas' options), the king and the questions to pick, the answers to vote. Lluvia's sky and its hint are on a dark grey panel too, on a computer as on a phone.
- **A paused round** (`PauseVeil`): "Partida en pausa" covers only the dark grey panel where the round is answered, with its round corners and over everything on it (the map's "Confirmar pin" and zoom too); the card stays in sight. In every game alike; on a central device and a phone beside it, the whole stage.
- **The scoreboard** (`PartyPanel scores`; the same look during a game's roulette and tutorial, `.tv-room.is-intro`, so it doesn't change when the rounds start): "MARCADOR" and a pill with how the round goes ("Respondieron 3/4", "Confirmaron", "Fijaron", "Siguen", "Votaron", "En juego"), a flat box per player (position, Blob with a green dot once they answered, your name alone (never a "tú"), a quiet line: "Respondió", "Confirmó", "Pensando…", "Buscando…", "6 aciertos", "Ya votó", "Rey" in yellow, "Retador"; points with a dot for thousands), you with a yellow ring. No code nor crown in it, also once the match is over; then each player's line is only their right answers ("1 acierto", "6 aciertos").
- **The bottom bar** (`MatchBar`): everyone's Blob (a green dot once they answered), who the round still waits for, you by your name too ("Faltan Pablo y Lynz por confirmar", "Faltan 3 por responder"; Campo de minas who stepped on a mine, Rey "Nadie le ha quitado la corona a Lynz todavía"), then "Reportar" and "Pausar" (the host), grey. No "Terminar" there: the host's "Salir" already ends the match for everyone (it stays on a phone beside a central device).
- **Each game, as its board:** Encuentra el país: "+" and "−" on the map top left (no strip of regions), the sea and land in the game's teal, "Confirmar pin" always so named; the question says which mode it is: "¿Dónde está Perú?", "¿De qué país es esta silueta?", "¿De qué país es esta bandera?", with the silhouette or the flag on its left (Trivia's "Imágenes" questions put their picture or flag in the same place). Rango: "TU RESPUESTA" and the number big on its right, the bar (filled in orange, a white square knob) between "−" and "+", "Fijar respuesta". Campo de minas: no counters over the board; a taken cell like Trivia's options at the reveal (right solid green, a mine red, each with its darker edge), a white square with the check or the bomb top right and the Blob of whoever took it bottom left; the card over the board is a panel over the page (no line, a soft drop shadow) with the board darkened behind it. Rey: the king big on the left ("El rey · eligió esta") and "RETADORES" on the right. Farsante: "Escribir · Votar · Revelar" on the card, your answer with a pencil and "La escribiste tú". Letra: the gap a pink dashed box with "?".
- **Phones (narrower than 900 px, or shorter than 500 px): one frame that is exactly the screen, nothing scrolls** (`lib/useMatchViewport.js`, `html.tv-match-lock`; at the end of `look.css`). The same in every game, Lluvia too, and in the intro before them. Top to bottom:
  - **The top bar** keeps "Salir" (always there, in every game), "RONDA 4 DE 10" with its segments (the game is on the card), the volume and your Blob. In the intro the same place says "CLÁSICO · JUEGO 2" with one segment per game.
  - **The card** at `--qc-h` (26 % of the height, 148 to 184 px): the same place and size in every round. The question at the bottom of what's left, over the time bar, whole. The small one (Encuentra el país, Campo de minas, Lluvia) is 118 px, so the map or the board gets the room.
  - **The answers** take all that's left: four options one under the other (two columns from 600 px), the panel to write in, Rango's number with its four buttons under it, the board, the map (it starts closer than the whole world, `START_ZOOM` in `WorldMap.jsx`; "−" or a pinch show the whole world, and the verdict goes on it instead of "+" and "−"), the king and the questions, the answers to vote (56 px at the least, scrolling inside when there are many).
  - **At the foot** the scoreboard as a strip of chips (`ScoreStrip` in `PartyPanel`: face, name and points in the ranking's order, a green dot once they answered, you with the yellow ring, sideways when they don't fit, keeping you in sight). A tap opens all of it in a panel ("Marcador", with how the round goes and each player's menu). Beside the strip "Reportar" and "Pausar" as icons; "¿Enviar reporte?" shows its word while it asks.
  - **The intro uses the same places, so nothing jumps when the round starts:** the roulette's panel and the tutorial's name are where the card goes (its bar counting down the tutorial), the three steps where the answers go, the strip at the foot. The game before fills that place too. Into the first round only what changes fades in.
  - **The keyboard:** the frame follows `visualViewport`, so the box sits right above it. With it up (`html.tv-keyboard-up`) the card shrinks to 104 px and the strip, the tools and the line of what to do go, so the question and the box stay in sight. Without the keyboard, what a panel holds (the countdown, the box to write in) sits in its middle; with it up, at its top, over the keyboard.
  - **On its side:** the card on the left, the answers on the right, the strip under them.

## Colours and surfaces
- Page `--tv-bg` #0a0b0c with its faint pattern of triangles, circles, crosses and waves (`--tv-page-bg`).
- Big panels (a panel over the page, the group's bar) `--tv-surface` #141517, 20–24 px corners, no coloured border, no edge; over the page only a soft drop shadow, and the page behind is darkened (60 % black).
- Boxes inside them (option rows, the code's box) `--tv-surface-2` #1c1d20, flat, 12–14 px corners.
- Games: Trivia `--tv-qm`, Adivina la canción `--p-music-*`, Encuentra el país `--p-world-*`, Rango `--tv-hm`, Campo de minas `--tv-mn`, Lluvia `--tv-rn`, Rey `--tv-ry`, Farsante `--tv-fs`; each with its darker `--edge`, never black.
- Chosen (an option of a row, a category): the site's blue `--tv-pick` (#2f6bff) with `0 3px 0 --tv-pick-edge` (#1a3c99), white text; the others have no fill. Text links `--tv-link` (#5b8cff), bold, never underlined.

## Type
- Titles (a game's name, a panel's title): Outfit 800, tighter (`--tv-title`, `-0.03em`).
- Buttons, game names and labels: Baloo 2, 700–800 (`--tv-display`).
- Quiet text (subtitles, notes): Plus Jakarta Sans (`--tv-small`), grey `--tv-text-3`.

## Pieces
- **The game's stage** (`ModeStage`, `.tv-stage`) is the middle of Inicio, of the group's room and of the search: the game's **card** in its colour (`.tv-stage-card`, flat: only its "Ajustes" is tapped; 24 px corners) and, beside it, two dark boxes (`.tv-stage-aside`), the whole as wide as the group's bar and coming in as one panel. **The card:** the game's icon on a white square and, in one row beside it, who the match is for in small (`.tv-stage-aud`: globe "Partida pública" or lock "Partida privada") over the game's name, always on one line ("…" if even the smallest size doesn't fit). At the bottom of the card (`.tv-stage-bottom`) three short flat lines (`.tv-stage-lines`, an icon on a lighter square and a few words): how it's played (the game's `tagline`), the settings (`settings.summary`; on Inicio a private match shows a new one's, `privateMatchLine` in `matchInfo.js`, the same words as its "Ajustes" tabs, never "Ajustes por defecto"), and how many play it now (a white dot sending out a ring). In a private match that last line is the glass "Ajustes" button (or who chooses them, with a lock). A public match says nothing about its rules. The search shows its rows there instead (time, queue, players found). The game's icon, big and faint, is cut by the bottom right corner. **Beside it:** "Tu elo" (the number with a yellow star and "+38 esta semana", green, red when it went down) with **your character** in its empty right side: "Blob" (`components/home/Blob.jsx`), in your colour, its eyes looking around (neutral); where phones keep "Reto del día" (a yellow square with a flame, the goal and how, "4/10" and a yellow bar; on a computer the challenge is in the bar below); last one box (`.tv-stage-social`) titled "Tu grupo" ("Tu grupo · N" with others in): you first, with your name and Blob, and the others, in the rows of "Tu grupo" (a tap unfolds that player's options under their name); under the last row "Invitar" (`.tv-stage-group-row`, with the red "Salir del grupo" icon to its left once others are in); under a thin line "Amigos en línea" (every online friend with "Unirme" and "Invitar" stuck to the right of the row (`.tv-stage-friends-btns`); without an account, a line saying what it's for). It scrolls inside when long. It isn't shown on phones (≤ 760 px): there the group is in the top bar and the friends in "Amigos". No "Crear grupo" nor box for a code. The group's code is never there (only in "Tu grupo"). The elo, the players and the challenge are example numbers until those systems exist (`lib/gameStats.js`). On phones (≤ 760 px) Inicio fits one screen with no scrolling (from about 700 px of height): the card drops its long description (`.tv-stage-intro`) and shrinks its head and lines, (on a phone 800 px tall or more the description comes back), the card takes any spare height so there is no empty space above the bar (its three lines keep their normal spacing and sit at the card's foot, the empty space between the head and them), and "Reto del día" (left) and the elo (right) share one row, half and half. Narrower than 860 px the card goes across the top and the boxes under it (one under the other below 560 px). A Clásico's card is in the logo's look (`.is-classic`: dark in the logo's coloured frame, the logo in place of the icon), with the same boxes beside it (its elo is the general one). A private match before its host picks: "Elige un juego" in `--tv-surface`, with the boxes beside it ("Tu elo", the general one).
- **The games' cards** (`GameCard`, `.tv-gcard`): tapped, so with their edge (`0 4px 0 var(--edge)`); icon on a white square top left, name and tagline, the faint icon cut by the corner; the chosen one with a white ring. 4 columns on a computer, 2 on phones.
- **"Clásico"** (`.tv-ccard`, first in "¿Qué quieren jugar?", above "Juego libre"): one tile half the panel wide (two of the games’ columns; the whole width on phones), like the one it had on the old room screen: black, tapped (edge `--tv-logo-shadow`), in a 5 px frame of the logo’s five coloured slices (`--logo-1…5`). "Clásico" and "Primero en ganar dos juegos" at the top left (`.tv-ccard-name`, `-sub`, which stop short of the logo) and the logo (`AppIcon`) big on the right, tilted and cut by the bottom right corner (`.tv-ccard-logo`), wiggling on hover. Chosen, the games' white ring (`.is-selected`). Its big card (Inicio, its lobby, its search) is the same stage in the logo's look (`.tv-stage.is-classic`: dark in the logo's coloured frame, the logo where a game's badge goes and "Primero en ganar dos juegos" under its name; its tutorial says how it goes).
- **"Vas a jugar"** (the group's bar, `.tv-gbar-plan`): the same card (`.tv-gcard` and its badge, name and faint icon), in the game's colour and with its icon, smaller and always 250 px wide on a computer whatever the game's name (long ones cut with "…"), who it's for in small over the name (`.tv-gbar-plan-aud`: globe and "Partida pública", or lock and "Partida privada"), a chevron on the right; grey "Elige un juego" with no game. The Clásico's is in the logo's look (`.is-classic`: black in the logo's coloured frame, the logo where a game's badge goes). It's where the game and public or private are changed, both in "¿Qué quieren jugar?" (no strip or list of games on the screen, no switch beside it). Alone in its PARTIDA box (`.tv-gbar-plan-row`).
- **Panels over the page** (`Modal.jsx`, `.tv-modal`): title and a grey X; a body that scrolls and an optional foot (Restablecer left, Listo right). On phones a sheet from the bottom. Inside `.tv-app` (its styles reach them). **The keyboard** (`useKeyboardFit`, for `Modal` and the sheets with a box to write in: the name, Amigos): only while a box inside the panel is being written in on a touch screen does the panel fit the part of the screen the keyboard leaves (`is-keyboard`), and then its colour runs on below it, past the screen's foot, so the page never shows behind the keyboard or the iPhone's see-through bar over it; otherwise it takes the whole screen (the iPhone sometimes keeps the smaller size after the keyboard closes). Boxes to write in are at least 16 px on phones: with less, the iPhone zooms the page in when one is tapped.
- **Pills** (`.tv-pill`, "Partida pública / Partida privada") and **tabs** (`SettingsTabs`: icon and title, a grey summary under, the open one with a 2 px blue line): flat.
- **No profile photos: every player's face is their Blob** (`Avatar.jsx` draws `Blob.jsx` in the colour of their `avatar`, with a black line), at any size; yours in the group's bar has a yellow ring.
- **The profile** keeps only what matters: your Blob, name, @username and two tags at most ("Owner", "Registrado"); "Estadísticas" with Victorias and Aciertos and "Color de tu Blob" under them; "Tu cuenta" (name, @username, Google to link, Spotify for owners, sign out). No match history.
- **Results** fit the panel with no scrolling: side by side the cards give up height when it's short, and below 860 px of height the head, podium, rows and buttons are a little smaller (`home.css`).

## How big panels move
- Every big panel (the game's stage, the group's bar, a panel over the page, the room's column, a public match's card, the central lobby, profile cards) comes in all together, nothing inside it animating apart, up from below: `--tv-box-in` (`tvBoxIn`, in `home.css`'s `:root`). Going out is the reverse, all together: `--tv-box-out` (`tvBoxOut`, 0.3 s). Reduced motion: none.
- Inicio's card comes in once, with the screen. After that (a game chosen, a sala made or left, public or private, a search) it only updates: no entrance of the box, its head or its lines (`.is-settled` in `look.css`, set by `HomeFrame`). Its sweep of light (`.tv-mstage-flash`) stays, as the sign that something changed.
- Every panel over the page (`Modal`, `.tv-sheet`: confirmations, Amigos, the name, a match's summary; the owner's panel) moves the same: `--tv-box-in` / `--tv-box-out`, its backdrop fading in step, with no blur. Whatever unmounts one on a timer waits past the 0.3 s of the way out.

## Shadows
- **With a shadow** (a solid edge in the piece's own darker colour, `box-shadow: 0 3px 0 var(--edge)`): only what is tapped and does something. Buttons (`.tv-btn`, `.tv-iconbtn`, the top bar's `.tv-chip`), answers (`.tv-option`, `.tv-quiz-opt`), cells (`.tv-mines-cell`), the games' cards (`.tv-gcard`), chosen options (`.tv-optrow-btn` pressed), coloured icons. On hover it rises (1–2 px) and the edge grows; pressed, it goes down and the edge disappears.
- The edge is always the piece's colour, darker: `--edge` of its `tv-c-*`, `--p-accent-edge` (#b39200) for the yellow, `--tv-neutral-edge` (#191919) for grey, `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.22)` for a white button on a coloured card (`0.18` for the game card's glass "Ajustes"), `--p-bad-edge` for the red `.tv-btn--bad`. Never a plain black edge under a coloured or white button.
- Quiet icon buttons with no background (a row's X, a playlist row's tools) stay flat.
- Buttons are rounded rectangles: square icon ones 40 px with 12 px corners (36 px / 10 px small), text ones 12 px corners (10 px small, 14 px big). Only avatars, dots, switches and labels are round.
- **Flat:** boxes to write in (a coloured ring on focus), pills and tabs (the chosen one a lighter fill, `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`, or a line), cards and rows, labels, avatars, covers.
- **Never** an inset shadow at the top (`inset 0 3px 0 …`).
- Text buttons and profile links are never underlined: they brighten on hover.
