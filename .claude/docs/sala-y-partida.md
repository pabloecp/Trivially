# Rooms and the game loop (backend), Trivia and Adivina la canción

## Rooms
- `game/roomManager.js` keeps every room in memory: a `Map` by code plus `socketToRoom` (socket id → `{code, userId}`). Nothing is persisted.
- A room is a match: a public one (`room.matchmaking`, no host) or a private one (yours alone, or a group's: `room.groupCode`, its host is the group's leader). The players who go together from one match to the next are a **group**, apart from the rooms (`grupos.md`). A private room outlives its games ("Volver a jugar", "Volver al lobby"). `room.game` is a `GAME_IDS` id (`null` only in an older room: the frame then shows "Elige un juego"). Only the host changes it (`setGame`, socket `room:setGame`, from "¿Qué quieren jugar?"), which abandons the match (`resetMatch`).
- The room's screen while it waits is the frame of Inicio (`HomeFrame.jsx`): the game's own lobby (`modes/*/LobbyPanel.jsx`: `ModeStage` + `SettingsPanel`) over the group's bar (`GroupBar.jsx`), which has the start button (JUGAR, `useStartGame`; the same word as a public match's). The settings hide behind a summary in the stage's right column ("5 rondas · 15 s · Todas las categorías", the tabs' notes, with the "Ajustes" button under it: each `LobbyPanel` passes its `ModeStage` as `SettingsPanel`'s `card`) and open in "Ajustes de Trivia" (`SettingsPanel` → `Modal.jsx`), every tab's part one after another (`SettingsTabs` stacked), with "Restablecer" (`room:config { reset: game }`, off while `settingsChanged` is false) and "Listo". The playlist keeps its settings beside its card (`SettingsPanel inline`). The room's column (`PartyPanel`) is in "Tu grupo" (`GroupSheet.jsx`) while a group's private match waits, and beside the intro and the matches; it shows the group's code (`groupCode`), never the room's.
- When the host gives or takes back permission to change the settings (`room.coHosts`, the player's menu), that player gets a toast saying so (`HomeFrame`).
- While a room waits (a private match before it starts, a search) it is Inicio for its players: Inicio's address (`/`, `roomPath`, `waitsOnInicio`), no "Volver" and no logo in the top bar, so the code never shows in the address. The bar changes it (another game; "Partida pública" goes back to Inicio to search) or cancels the search. Only the central device has "Volver" there. In a match the "X" asks first: the group's leader takes the whole group to Inicio (`group:home`), anyone else goes alone (still in their group). A game's "Terminar" and the host's X in a match go back to the room's lobby in the same game (`room:restart`, `restartGame`), not to a menu.
- `leave()` removes a player at once; `disconnect()` keeps the seat and host role `RECONNECT_GRACE_MS` (20 s), and `addPlayer()` with the same user id reclaims it. The room dies when the last connected player goes.
- **A match's screen** is the same frame in every game but Lluvia, which keeps its own (`lluvia.md`) (`components/home/MatchFrame.jsx`, see `diseno.md`, "A match"): the round's card (`MatchCard`), what to answer with under it, the scoreboard (`PartyPanel scores`, its `status` pill) and the bottom bar (`MatchBar`: who's missing, Reportar, Pausar, Terminar). The top bar shows the game, its mode (`matchMode`) and the round. A central device and the phones beside it keep their own (`ControllerBar`).
- Host handover: `PlayerMenu.jsx` "Hacer anfitrión" (two taps) → `room:giveHost` / `giveHost`; the old host becomes a co-host (`transferHost`).

## Phases
- A private match of one game starts with its "Cómo se juega" (phase `intro`, `INTRO_TUTORIAL_MS`, no roulette; `MatchIntro`), so every game has it. Only with `tutorials: true` (`index.js`): tests go straight to the rounds.
- `lobby → countdown (3 s) → playing (config.roundMs) → reveal → next countdown … → finished`. The reveal is 3 s (`REVEAL_MS`) except Campo de minas (`MINES_REVEAL_MS`, 6 s), Lluvia (`RAIN_REVEAL_MS`, 5 s), Rey (4 s) and Farsante (8 s).
- `setPhase()` owns the single `room.timer`; every transition calls `rooms.onPhaseChange`, which `sockets.js` wires to broadcast `room:state`; `watchRoom()` re-broadcasts about once a second during a match.
- "Volver a jugar" = `room:replay` (same game and settings); "Volver al lobby" = `room:restart`.
- **Pause** (`room:pause`, `pause`/`resume`, `canPause`: the host, or a verified owner in any match): `room.paused.remainingMs` freezes the time, `assertNotSpectator` refuses answers, resuming restarts `room.phaseOnEnd` and shifts `phaseStartedAt`. UI: `PauseButton`/`PauseVeil` in `components/home/PauseControls.jsx`.
- **Report** (`ReportButton`, `room:report`, `questionReport`): once per round per player; saved by `db/reports.js` to Supabase `question_reports`, else `backend/private/question-reports.jsonl`.
- **Skip** (owners, `SkipButton`, `room:skipRound`, `skipRound`): grades the round, skips its reveal and the next countdown.

## publicState
`publicState(room, forUserId)` is the only serializer. Answers, picks and the track's metadata are hidden until `reveal` (during playing only `audio.previewUrl`). The `me` block only goes in ack responses (`forUserId`), not broadcasts.

## Answers and scoring
- `game:answer` takes a track id or free text (`isCorrectAnswer` in `game/answers.js`: accents, parentheticals, Levenshtein, substrings).
- `game/scoring.js`: a right answer 500 + up to 500 for speed; closeness rounds 1000 exact, less when near (`locationPoints`, `yearPoints`, `figurePoints`). Streaks are stats only.
- **Inactive:** `markIdle` counts rounds without an answer (`idleRounds`); after `IDLE_ROUNDS` (3) the player is `idle`: not waited for, "Inactivo" on the board. Any answer resets it.
- **Spectators:** joining mid-match sets `player.spectator`: they see rounds, can't answer (`assertNotSpectator`), are never waited for (`isPlaying`), and are out of ranking, stats and tiebreaks. `resetMatch()` clears it.

## After a match
- `applyMatchStats` writes per-user stats; `matchWinner`: first place with points, a tie counts for nobody unless a tiebreak settled it.
- History: `user.history` (newest first, `MATCH_HISTORY_LIMIT` 10). Question ids never go out: `publicHistory`, `sanitizeUser` (own account, with rounds), `sanitizeUserPublic` (never rounds), `publicRounds`. UI: `MatchHistory.jsx`, `MatchSummarySheet`.
- Summary: `recordRound` fills `player.rounds` (`{ round, game, style?, prompt, answer, yours, points, status }`, `style` only where the game has modes: Encuentra el país, Adivina la canción), sent as `results[].rounds`. `status` is the same colour in every game: `ok` green (won it all), `near` yellow (scored but didn't win: a mine with right cells too), `bad` red (lost with no points, also what was written or placed and went in on its own when the time ran out), `none` grey (no answer, a question skipped, or a mines board the time ran out on, with no points). Campo de minas says how the board ended and, only if there were some, how many right cells. UI: `MatchSummarySheet`, the game's card (its colour and icon, "Varios juegos" in a playlist or Clásico) and five rounds at a time with arrows at the foot.
- Results screens share `components/home/ResultsParts.jsx` (`PodiumCard`, `RetouchCard` from `ResultsLooks.jsx`, `GamesWon`). One column below 720 px. On phones the buttons go in `ResultsFoot`, a bar pinned to the bottom like `.tv-roomdock` (portal into `.tv-app`; the way back is a square with its icon), and the podium shows only first names (`.tv-podium-first`). With more than five players the ranking shows five whole rows and scrolls inside for the rest (`useFiveRows`, `.tv-res-table.is-five`, snapping to whole rows).
- Phones: during an intro (`.tv-mcard`) the scoreboard stays under the card (home.css, "Phones: the room's bars").

## Room extras
- **No chat:** the room has none (it was removed). A sala (a group and its private match) holds at most `SALA_MAX` (10) players (`matchmaking.js`; `groups.js addMember`, `RoomManager.addPlayer`). Scoreboards and lists of players come in with no animation (`look.css`, end); only the final podium keeps its own.
- **Hidden code** (`lib/hiddenCode.js`, key `trivially_hide_code`): dots on this device, and the URL uses `HIDDEN_SLUG` "oculta" instead of the code (`urlCode`, `resolveCode`, `RoomNavigator`).

## Trivia (`opciones`, `QUIZ_GAME`)
- `config.quiz`: `rounds` 5–25, `roundMs` 15–35 s, and the ticked lists `categories`, `formats` (`opciones|escribir`) and `difficulties`; an empty list blocks the start.
- `game/quiz.js` `quizPool()`: bank `multiple_choice` and `open` rows, plus the capitals and flags of `geoBank()`. Every four-option question is also a written one (`asWritten`: id `<id>:escribir`, `data.aliases`, the other options in `reject`); questions needing the options ("¿Cuál de los siguientes…?") are left out, and a match never draws both versions (`sameQuestion`).
- `pickQuizQuestions` shares the rounds between kinds; `choice` answers are recorded by `submitQuizAnswer` and graded together by `gradeQuiz()` at the reveal; `open` ones go through `isCorrectOpenAnswer` (`isChoiceRound`).
- A written answer (Trivia, Adivina la canción) left in its box when the time runs out goes on its own half a second before the end (`WriteRound` and the song's box, from `useRemainingMs`), as if "Enviar" had been pressed (the song's: the highlighted suggestion, else what was typed).
- `publicQuestion()` never sends the id; options and flag from `playing`, answer and `picks` at `reveal`. The lobby gets `questionCounts`: what each choice has with the other lists, where a list left empty filters nothing (otherwise, with two lists empty, nothing could be ticked again).
- Frontend `src/modes/quiz/` (`/quiz/:code`), styles `styles/quiz.css`.

## Adivina la canción (`musica`)
- `config.formats` `opciones|escribir` (`MUSIC_FORMATS`). `pickAhead` + `withFormat()` give each round a way: open (search box) or `choice` (four "Título - Artista" songs, by the same artist when possible; `artistsOf` splits artists but keeps `ONE_ARTIST` acts whole).
- Closed rounds use Trivia's `isChoiceRound`/`submitQuizAnswer`/`gradeQuiz`. Autocomplete list: `GET /api/songs` (`globalSongList()`), plus `searchCatalog` with the room's extras during `playing`.
- **Default playlists** (`catalog/defaultPlaylists.js`): offered in every room's "Tus playlists", unticked, first in the list, never deletable (`removeSpotifyPlaylist`), kept loaded in every room (`ensureDefaultPlaylists`, `applyDefaultPlaylists`; a lobby room gets a newer `version`). `publicState` marks them `defaultFrom` ("Spotify" | "Lynz"). Two: Spotify's **Top 50: Global** (read from its public page once a day; also listed by the frontend's `DEFAULT_PLAYLISTS`) and **Top 100: Lynz**.
- **Top 100: Lynz** (`catalog/lynz.js`): the 100 best-rated songs of Lynz, `GET <LYNZ_API_URL>/api/rankings/top-songs` (no sign-in; without `LYNZ_API_URL` the playlist isn't offered). Lynz allows **2 requests a week per IP** (429 + `Retry-After`), so `LynzTop.refresh` asks once a week, keeps the answer with the time of every ask (Supabase table `feed_cache`, else `backend/private/lynz-top-songs.json`, `LYNZ_CACHE_FILE` in tests) and never asks more than twice in any week, restarts included; a 429 waits its `Retry-After`, another failure retries after 12 h and keeps the old list. Never call the API anywhere else (a page, a script): it would spend the same quota.
- Lynz gives only title and artists, so each song gets the id `lz-<hash of title+artists>` (`lynzSongId`, stable every week) and goes through the `TrackResolver` like a pasted playlist's (catalog → `spotify_songs` → iTunes). `warmLynz` does it in the background when the list is new: every song once, then a deeper pass for the ones missed, so ticking the playlist later is instant; the best-ranked song found gives the playlist its cover.
- Frontend `modes/music/Game.jsx`.
- **The catalog grows with Spotify** (`catalog/catalogGrowth.js`): a song of a Spotify playlist or album played in a room, once found on iTunes, is checked in the background; if it isn't in the catalog (same iTunes id, preview, or title + main artist) and has ≥ 250 million plays (`MIN_STREAMS`, from its main artist's kworb.net page; the artist from Spotify's embed page of the song), it is saved in Supabase's `songs` table with id `sp-<spotifyId>` and joins the catalog in memory at once (rankings and difficulty worked out again). Language: the iTunes genre, else the artist's other catalog songs, else English. Only with Supabase. `npm run catalog:upload` never deletes `sp-…` songs.
- **Modos de juego** (`config.styles`, `MUSIC_STYLES`): `titulo` (guess the song) and `letra` (**Letra** on screen; "Completa la letra" in the code's comments and below). A new room has only `titulo` (`DEFAULT_MUSIC_STYLES`; an older room without the list too), so public matches and Clásicos never play it. With both ticked `nextStyle` shares the rounds evenly, and a `letra` round with no fresh ready song plays `titulo`. Only `letra` ticked: `songsReady` counts only the songs with a round; the lobby also gets `lyricsReady` (the chip can't be ticked at 0).
- **Completa la letra**: rounds are worked out **once, offline, on a computer** (`npm run lyrics:build`, `scripts/buildLyrics.js` + `scripts/lyrics/transcribe.py` in `backend/private/lyrics-tools/.venv`: faster-whisper, large-v3 on the GPU, else medium on the CPU) and the server only reads them (`lyrics/lyricRounds.js`, at start: `backend/private/lyric-rounds.json`, and Supabase's `lyric_rounds` table with `LYRICS_DB=1`; `npm run lyrics:upload` fills it). Nothing is worked out or asked for while playing. **Never commit lyrics** (rights; public repo).
  - The build (`lyrics/roundBuilder.js`): the song's own preview (catalog: iTunes'; the default Spotify playlists and `--spotify` links: Spotify's, id `sp-<spotifyId>` like `linkSong`) transcribed by Whisper with each word's time, matched word by word to LRCLIB's lyrics (`alignWords`, Smith–Waterman: Whisper is only trusted for times, the text is LRCLIB's). `pickRound`: a word heard clearly (probability ≥ 0.6), never a stopword, ≥ 8 s into the preview, with two words before it in its line, the last word of a line above any other (another word only when no last word fits), then a chorus line; cut 80 ms before it. **The line shown ends at the gap** (`endAtGap`: "Y yo que hasta ayer solo fui un ___", never text after it) and **the song doesn't play after the cut** until the reveal. Then the cut is **checked** by transcribing again: nothing of the answer (or its start) in the 4 s before (else 0.1 s earlier, up to 3 times), and the answer among the first words after. Songs that don't line up or fail are kept as `data: null` with their `why` (`--retry` tries them again, `--why` only those of one reason, `--engine alt` with `transcribe-alt.py`: the voice separated from the music, then wav2vec2). Every version of the lyrics in LRCLIB is tried, best match first. `--redo-before <date>` does them all again and can be stopped and resumed (each row keeps when it was `built`).
  - A row: `song_id`, `spotify_id`, `preview_url`, `preview_etag`, `data` = only `{ lines: [{ at, text }], cut, end, answer, wrong }`. Rounds built before may still have text after the gap plus `back` and `stop`: `toVerse` cuts the line at the gap and ignores those two, so they need no rebuild. A round plays only with **its own audio**: `RoomManager.lyricFor` checks the song's `previewUrl`, and its ETag once the server downloaded it (`mediaCache.etagOf`): a changed preview skips the song.
  - `withLyric` puts `track.style = "letra"` and `track.lyric` (`cutMs`, `startMs` = `LYRICS_LEAD_MS` 10 s before, `revealFromMs` = `LYRICS_REPLAY_MS` 3 s before the cut, `revealMs`). The round lasts that bit of song plus `config.roundMs` (`lyricLeadMs`). Open: `isCorrectOpenAnswer`; closed: answer + wrong ones through `gradeQuiz`. `publicState` sends `lyrics: { lines, startMs, cutMs, roundMs, revealFromMs, answerEndMs, answer }` (`answer` only at the reveal; nothing says how long it is).
  - The client (`modes/music/lyricAudio.js`) plays it with **Web Audio**, decoded during the countdown, from `startMs` to the cut to the sample, fading over the last 30 ms; at the reveal from `revealFromMs` on. `LyricsPanel`: every line in its place, each word lighting up like a karaoke (its line's time shared evenly), the gap one fixed-width line filled in place at the reveal. The audio proxy answers byte ranges.
