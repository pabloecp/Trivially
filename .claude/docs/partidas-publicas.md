# Public matches (Casual and Competitivo)

## Inicio and the group (`components/home/HomeFrame.jsx`)
- Inicio (`/`), a private match while it waits and the search for a public match are **one frame**, all on `/` (`roomPath`: no "Volver" and no code in the address while a room waits): the game's stage in the middle (`ModeStage`: the game and its tutorial, you with your elo, and the match: who it's for and how many play or its settings) and the bar at the bottom (`GroupBar.jsx`: without a group "Crear grupo" and, beside it, "Unirse" with a code (one box, white button); with one the red "Salir del grupo" (it asks first), "Tu grupo" with its faces and "+"; one grey box with "Partida pública" or "Partida privada" (with its icon, as plain text) and, right of it, "Vas a jugar", the game's own card at a fixed width; the one yellow button). "Arriba la info, al centro el juego, abajo tu grupo y la acción." Groups: `grupos.md`.
- **The plan** (`lib/plan.js`): the game and who it's for, `audience` **"publica"** ("Partida pública": a public match of that game) or **"grupo"** ("Partida privada": yours alone or your group's). Alone on Inicio it's this device's (`localStorage` `trivially_plan`, `useLocalPlan`); in a group on Inicio it's `group.plan` (leader only, `group:plan`); in a private match it's `room.game`. Remembered for Inicio (`rememberPlan`).
- **"¿Qué quieren jugar?"** ("¿Qué quieres jugar?" alone; `PlanSheet.jsx`, from the "Vas a jugar" button): two pills (the audience), "Clásico" and under it "Juego libre" with the games as cards (`GameCard.jsx`); with "Partida pública" Rey de la colina and Farsante are locked ("Partida privada", `PUBLIC_GAMES`). "Clásico" is a wide tile on both sides, in the logo's look (`.tv-ccard`): the roulette's games until someone has won two (`clasico.md`), a public match of them or a private one; the playlist is no longer offered here. Whoever doesn't decide only looks ("Lo elige Ana").
- On Inicio: JUGAR (publica) → `findMatch("casual", …, game)` (a group's leader: for the whole group; the Clásico searches without a game: the roulette's); picking a game with "grupo", or JUGAR, creates the private match (`createRoom`; JUGAR also starts it; the button says JUGAR on both sides), and a group goes into it. In a private match, switching to "Partida pública" goes back to Inicio (the whole group, `group:home` with the plan) to search.
- The old pages `/jugar/casual`, `/jugar/competitivo`, `/jugar/privada` open Inicio on that side (`OPTION_AUDIENCE` in `Home.jsx`). Leaving a room goes to Inicio (`exitPath`). Without an account `PlaySheet.jsx` asks the name first (`askName`, `onNamed`) and HomeFrame then does what was asked. **Competitivo is closed for now** (the server refuses it via `MATCH_KINDS_OPEN`); its rank code stays.

## Server (`game/matchmaking.js`, tests `matchmaking.test.js`)
- `match:find { kind, size }` → `RoomManager.findMatch`: a lobby room with `room.matchmaking` and **no host** (nobody changes, pauses or ends it); not joinable by code or friends.
- **Only 5 players for now:** "Uno contra Uno" (2) isn't offered (`DUELS_OPEN` false in `matchmaking.js`; `hidden` in the client's `MATCH_SIZES`, so `PlaySheet` shows no size picker). Its code stays: an explicit `size: 2` still makes a duel (the tests use it). Setting `DUELS_OPEN` back to true brings back the game queue's two and the group's "one more than the group".
- **By game** (`match:find { kind, game }`, "Partida pública"): a queue per game (`matchmaking.game`; `PUBLIC_GAMES`: Trivia, Adivina la canción, Encuentra el país, Rango, Campo de minas, Lluvia), for five unless `size` says otherwise (two with `DUELS_OPEN`). It plays that game alone in its public way (`PUBLIC_VARIANT`: Trivia Cerradas, Rango Cifras, Campo de minas carrera, and the variants `cancion`, `pais`, `lluvia`, which the roulette never draws), one item (`publicGameItem`, with easy questions if need be), **no decider**, and its intro is only the tutorial (`introSpinMs` 0). `publicMatchmaking` sends `game`.
- **A group together** (the leader's `match:find`, `grupos.md`): `findMatch(user, input, party)` looks for a room of that queue with a seat for each of them (or makes one), all in the same room, of `groupMatchSize` (5; with `DUELS_OPEN`, one more than the group, 2–5; at most `PUBLIC_GROUP_MAX` people); strangers and bots fill the rest. Nobody's group owns a public room: when it's over the group's leader takes it back to Inicio (`group:home`) or to another match, and the strangers stay with their results. Tests `game/publicGroup.test.js`, `realtime/groups.test.js`.
- Full → `checkMatchFull` waits `MATCH_FULL_MS` (3 s) → `startMatch`. Without a game (the roulette's, "Clásico"): variants from `MATCH_VARIANTS` (`trivia-cerradas`, `trivia-abiertas`, `rango-cifras`, `minas-carrera`), 5 rounds each, played as a playlist in order, **until someone has won two** (`MATCH_WINS`): two are played for sure, the next one joins after each game nobody has won the match in (`beginNextGame`). The whole rule is in `clasico.md`.
- **Games won decide it:** `scoreMatchGame` (`run.gameWins`, `run.gameBase`). `rankedPlayers`/`matchWinner` go by games won, then points; equal games won = no winner.
- Own clock (`matchTimes`, `matchRoundMs`): 10 s a question, 15 s a song or country, 10 s a mines turn, 30 s a race or shower.
- `MATCH_SIZE` env var (1–5) caps the size (1: test alone).
- **Intro** before each game: phase `intro` (`INTRO_MS`: roulette + tutorial, `VARIANT_TUTORIALS` in `matchInfo.js`); from the second game, the previous game's result first (`INTRO_RESULT_MS`, `run.lastGame`, `PreviousGame`).
- `publicPlayer` sends `score` as this game's points (`score - run.gameBase`) and `wins`.

## Rank (Competitivo)
`game/rank.js`: `user.rank` `{ points, matches, wins, best }` moved by place (`PLACE_POINTS`), never below 0. Tiers `RANK_TIERS` (Bronce 0 … Maestro 1500, divisions of `DIVISION_POINTS` 100), mirrored in `frontend/src/lib/rank.js`. Tests `rank.test.js`.

## Elo, matchmaking and rank (Casual)
- **Elo per game** (`game/elo.js`, tests `elo.test.js`): `user.elo[game]` `{ rating, games, best }`, start 500, moved by every game of a public match (`RoomManager.rateGame`, from `scoreMatchGame`): a single game's queue or each game of a Clásico. Each pair is a small duel by that game's points; guests play as 1000 and keep nothing. The profile sends `elo`; Inicio's "Tu elo" reads it (`eloIn`; the Clásico's is the average of its games).
- **Matchmaking by elo:** `findMatch` only joins a room whose humans' average elo is within `ELO_WINDOW` (300), widening `ELO_WIDEN_BY` every `ELO_WIDEN_EVERY_MS` the room has waited (`eloFits`).
- **Rank** (`rank.js`) now moves in the Casual's Clásico too (`rankedMatch`), by place.
- **Same rivals:** `user.together[rivalId]` counts public matches between two accounts; from `TOGETHER_LIMIT` (5) on they stop counting for each other's elo, and rank points are scaled by the share of rivals that still count (`rivalDiscount`).

## Reto del día (`game/daily.js`, tests `daily.test.js`)
- One challenge a day per account, drawn at random from 10 (`CHALLENGES`, each sized for 3 to 5 matches: play 4, win 3, podium 4 times, 20 right answers, 8.000 points, a streak of 3 in 3 matches, 4+ right in 3 matches, 3 public matches, 3 different games, 2.000+ points in 4 matches). It is the same all day and changes at midnight Mexico City time (`DAILY_TZ`).
- Every finished match of an account, in any game, public or private, adds to it (`applyDaily` from `applyMatchStats`). `user.daily` keeps today's progress and `user.dailyDone` how many challenges the account has completed in all; the account's data sends `daily` (today's with its goal, `target`, `progress`, `done`, `total`) and the public profile `dailyDone`. Guests have none. Inicio's "Reto del día" box (`DailyBox`) shows today's; the total is the profile's "Retos del día" stat.

## Bots (Casual)
- Bots are fixed in-memory profiles (`game/bots.js`) that look like accounts or guests: guests white (`gst_` id), accounts white or an account colour (`usr_` id, with a profile page and friend requests that go nowhere). Each has an elo per game that moves like anyone's; `pickBots` takes ones close to the room's elo and `skillFor` turns it into difficulty.
- `scheduleBotArrival` adds a bot every second after `BOT_WAIT_MS` until full (`addBot`: `bot: true` server-only, guest-like id, a Spanish/English two-word name, `skill` 0.15–0.45). **Players must never be able to tell a bot from a person**: `bot` is never sent and no text mentions bots.
- `scheduleBots`/`botMove` act between `BOT_MIN_MS` and `BOT_MAX_MS` (3–8 s) into a round. A room with only bots closes (`hasPeople`). Bots get no stats.

## Leaving and inactivity (public only)
- Leaving mid-match (or dropping past the grace) is a forfeit (`forfeit`, "salió"/"inactivo"): never waited for, no wins, last place, can't come back. One left → they win (`endIfAlone`).
- After `PUBLIC_KICK_ROUNDS` (5) idle rounds, "¿Sigues ahí?" (`askStillThere`, `AFK_CONFIRM_MS` 15 s, `StillThere.jsx`, `room:present`); no answer → `afkExpire`. Tests `game/publicForfeit.test.js`. Private rooms never kick anyone.

## Client
- The search is HomeFrame's `SearchCard`: "Buscando jugadores de Trivia…" with how long (`useSearchStart`), the game's queue ("Fila de Trivia · 5 rondas · 10 s · Cerradas", `publicMatchLine` in `matchInfo.js`) and how many were found; once full, "¡Partida completa!" and "Empieza en 3". Its button is CANCELAR (grey): the group's `group:home` (the leader's; the others see "Buscando con Ana…"), or leaving the room.
- **One address for the whole match once it plays:** `/partida/CODE` (the search waits on `/`) (`isInlineMatch`, `roomPath`, `RoomScreen`/`GAME_SCREENS` in `App.jsx`); only the end is `/playlist/CODE`. `RoomNavigator` adds `tv-seamless` / `tv-seamless-side` on `<html>` for 1.4 s so screens swap without re-animating (lengths 0 s, not `none`).
- `MatchIntro.jsx` (`PanelRoulette`, tutorial), styles `styles/match.css`.
