# Groups (`game/groups.js`, tests `realtime/groups.test.js`)

A **group** is who goes together; a **room** is a match (its lobby, its rounds, its scoreboard). Nobody has a group on arrival: without one you play alone (a public match, or a private one only for you). "Sala" is no longer a word for players: it's "grupo" and "partida".

## Server
- `GroupManager` keeps groups in memory like the rooms (`groups` by code, `socketToGroup`). Codes come from `createRoomCode` and never repeat a room's or another group's.
- A group: `{ code, leaderId, members (Map: name, avatar, isGuest, connected, socketId), joinMode, joinRequests, plan: { game, audience }, roomCode }`. `publicGroup` is its only serializer (`group:state`, sent to each member's socket): members with `here` (with the group right now), `plan` ("Vas a jugar" on Inicio), `joinMode`, `joinRequests`, `place` (`{ code, public, game, own }`, null on Inicio). `place()` is the room at `roomCode` while a member is still in it.
- **The leader moves everyone** (sockets.js): `room:create` makes the group's private match (`room.groupCode`, host = leader) and `moveGroupTo` brings the others in; `match:find` gives everyone the group can take a seat in one public room (`findMatch(user, input, party)`, at most `PUBLIC_GROUP_MAX`, checked before anyone moves); `group:home` takes them back to Inicio from its private match, a search, or a finished public match (optional `plan`). `movable` skips the central device and members in the middle of a public match (they catch up on the next move). Each moved client hears `room:moved` (`{ code, state }`, or `{ code: null }` for Inicio). Only the leader can search, create or move (`assertLeader`).
- **Members on their own:** `room:leave` (a match's "X") goes to Inicio alone and stays in the group. `group:join` with the code (also "Ir con el grupo", and every reload) puts them where the group is (`follow`: into its private match, as a spectator if a round is on, or back to their own seat in its public one). `group:leave` also leaves its private match, except the last one (the group is gone and the match is theirs alone). Joining another group leaves the old one. `group:create` from someone whose old group only keeps their place (the app was closed) leaves that one for the new.
- **Leader = host of its private match:** `syncHost` after `group:leader` or a dropped leader's place running out; `room:giveHost` there makes the new host the leader; `room:kick` there also take them out of the group (`group:left`, reason "sacado"); `group:kick` does both.
- A dropped player keeps their place `GROUP_GRACE_MS` (20 s), the leader their role too; then `passLeader`, and the group remembers that leader in `formerLeader` for `LEADER_RETURN_MS` (5 min). If they join again in that time, `reclaimLeader` gives the lead (and the private match's host, `syncHost`) back to them. Not given back if the leader was chosen by hand since (`setLeader` clears it), if the former leader left or was kicked, or if the time ran out. Home shows a toast on every leader change. The same account still in the group from another live device (it has to answer `room:ping`): `offerRoom` (the group's private match) and the client joins it, which opens CentralOffer.
- Friends (`amigos-y-enlaces.md`): `friend:join` follows the group's `joinMode`, `friend:invite` sends the group's code, `group:answerJoin`, `group:joinMode`. FriendsHub shows a friend's `room.group`, `inRoom`, `inMatch`.

## Client
- `lib/store.jsx`: `group` (its code in `sessionStorage` `trivially_group`, per tab like the room), `createGroup`, `checkGroup`, `joinGroup`, `leaveGroup`, `groupHome`, `setGroupPlan`, `giveLeader`, `kickFromGroup`. `rejoin` does the group first (it can place the tab back in a room).
- **There is no "Crear grupo" button nor a box for a code**: "Invitar" makes the group (`createGroup`, at once; if the player has no name, the name is asked first), and then opens "Tu grupo" with the code. Choosing a game never makes one: without a group it's only this device's plan (a new device starts on Trivia), and on the private side it makes the private match for you alone. A group is joined with its link (`/grupo/CODE`) or from "Amigos en línea" ("Unirme"); there is no writing a code any more (`checkGroup` stays in the store, unused).
- The group is beside the game's card (`ModeStage`'s `Boxes`, with what `HomeFrame` gives through `GroupBoxContext`), on Inicio, in a private match's lobby and while searching; the bar's first zone is "Reto del día" instead (`DailyZone`). Under "Tu elo" there is one box, `SocialBox` in `ModeStage.jsx` (on phones the "Reto del día" goes between the two):
  - **Your people**, top of the box: you first (name and your Blob, the rows of "Tu grupo": the leader's "Líder", "En otra partida" or "Reconectando…", dimmed when not `here`), then the others. The title **"Tu grupo" is always on top** ("Tu grupo · N" once a second player is in); alone (no group, or a group of one) it is only you, tagged "Conectado". A tap on a row (only with a group) unfolds that player's options under their name, as on a match's scoreboard (`GroupMemberMenu.jsx`, the same `.tv-player-menu`): their profile (faded on a guest) and, for the leader on the others, icons for the settings' permission, taking them out of the group and making them the leader (taking out and making the leader ask for a second tap). "Tu grupo" itself opens from "Invitar".
  - **"Invitar"**, under the last row: with a group it opens "Tu grupo" (with the join requests' red count); without one it makes the group first (disabled while searching). With others in the group the red "Salir del grupo" icon button sits to its left (it only asks: `HomeFrame` shows `ConfirmDialog` "¿Seguro que quieres salir del grupo?" with "Quedarme" and "Salir del grupo", and leaves only on the second).
  - **"Amigos en línea"**, in the same box under a thin line: all the friends who are online, where each one is, and two small buttons stuck to the right of each row: "Unirme" (or "Pedir unirme" / "Esperando…" by the friend's group's `joinMode`; a friend with no group and no match can be asked too: `friend:join` makes them a group, with them as leader and in "solicitud", and sends them `friend:groupMade` (the store's `adoptGroup`) so they see it, with the request, in "Tu grupo"; off when they are in a match without a group or their group is only by invitation). A friend alone in a group on Inicio reads "En línea", not "En un grupo": the group shows in their line only with more than one in it (`statusOf`) and "Invitar" (sends them your group's link; without a group it makes one first, via `onMakeGroup`; "Invitado" for 15 s). Friends already in your group are not listed (the box says all of them are in it when that leaves none); without an account, the login buttons. The box scrolls when it gets long.
- **The code isn't on the bar nor beside the card**: "Tu grupo" (`GroupSheet.jsx`) is the only place a player sees it (titled "Enlace del grupo": the invite link itself, `host/grupo/CODE`, a container with only the label "Enlace del grupo", a "Mostrar" button ("Ocultar" once open) and, at the far right, the copy button; the link is hidden (nothing shows) every time the window opens; "Mostrar" swaps the label for the link itself in the same row, so the box never changes size,, which says "Enlace copiado" in a toast; if the clipboard fails the toast shows the link), then `GroupMembers`; the leader's `FriendsRoomControls` and `InviteFriends`. Phones: top left of the top bar (GroupMenu) there is always the container "Grupo" (its title and your Blob, and with a group the faces and how many; a tap opens "Tu grupo", with "Salir del grupo" at its foot, and without a group it does what "Invitar" does: makes it, then opens it) and an "Invitar" button beside it (the same as the box's: with a group it opens "Tu grupo", without one it makes it first). Once someone else is in the group "Invitar" is only a "+", and the container moves left to give room to "Salir del grupo" (asks first), the same size and place as the top bar's "Volver". Under the card the "Reto del día" (left) and the elo (right) share the row, half and half (the bar has no challenge there) and the people's box ("Tu grupo" with "Amigos en línea") is not shown: the group is in the top bar and the friends are in "Amigos".
- Invite link `/grupo/CODE` (`useInvite`; `/sala/CODE`, from the links of a while ago, works too; `Home.jsx` joins, asking for a name first, then goes to `/`). **Inicio is always `/`**: the group's code is never in the address, nor while the group's private match waits (`roomPath` is `/` for a waiting room); a match plays on its game's screens (`/partida/CODE` for a public match or a Clásico). `RoomNavigator` leaves a match's screens when the room is gone from under it.
- HomeFrame: non-leaders see "Esperando a Ana…" and a read-only "¿Qué quieren jugar?"; away from the group's private match, "Ir con el grupo"; while searching only the leader has CANCELAR ("Buscando con Ana…"). TvTopbar has no "Volver" while a room waits on Inicio (a private match or a search: the bar's own buttons change or cancel it); a match's "X": the leader where the group is = `group:home` ("Todo el grupo volverá al inicio contigo"), anyone else goes alone ("Seguirás en tu grupo"). Public results: the leader's "Volver" and "Buscar otra partida" are for everyone; the rest get "Volver" and "Esperando a Ana".
- **"Tu grupo" has one list** (`GroupMembers` in `GroupSheet.jsx`), with a private match or without one: on each row its buttons: for the leader, settings permission, take out and make leader; the leader's crown (yellow, not tappable); and profile for everyone (faded and doing nothing on a guest). A profile's address is its username (`/profile/name`; `GET /api/users/name`). Whoever is taken out (of the group or of its private match) can't come back, not even with the code (`group.kicked`, `room.kicked`). The permission is the group's (`group.settingsBy`, `group:permission`, `canEditConfig` in `publicGroup`): it can be given on Inicio and holds in its private matches (`syncPermissions`, into `room.coHosts`). The tap-a-player menu (`PlayerMenu`) is only on a match's scoreboard: the same buttons, as a row of icons, but during a match only taking out and the profile: permissions, the host and the leader only change while the room waits (the server refuses them otherwise). Whoever is made the leader gets a notice (`HomeFrame`).
